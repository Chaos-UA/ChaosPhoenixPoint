[h1]Chaos Inventory — No Items Teleport[/h1]
[i]No more weapons/armor/items teleporting from one side of the map to another! Now items are bound to a specific aircraft or base and can't be teleported.[/i]

[h2]What It Does[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]No cross-aircraft snooping[/b] — a soldier can equip only items on its Aircraft.
[*][b]Base ↔ aircraft transfer while docked[/b] — right-click any item in the Storage list to move it to any other reachable location: the docked base, or another docked aircraft.
[*][b]Global Inventory stays accessible[/b] — freshly manufactured items that can be instantly built like ammo/medkits etc can be assigned to any aircraft without docking to base, other items require docking to equip.
[*][b]Mission loot goes to Aircraft[/b] — items recovered from a mission go straight to the aircraft inventory.
[/list]

[h2]The Merged Storage View[/h2]
Every equip screen now shows items from [b]everywhere[/b] at once, color-coded so you always know what you're looking at and where each item is located:
[list]
[*][b]White[/b] — belongs to the aircraft/base of the soldier you're currently viewing.
[*][b]Light blue[/b] — equipable from here, but actually located in a docked aircraft/base.
[*][b]Red[/b] — not reachable right now. Click on it to see/navigate to where the item is located.
[/list]
The Air Force tab also shows each aircraft's claimed cargo, with its own filter, an "unload" menu, and a live cargo-count badge next to each aircraft's crew count.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] Should be safe to disable.
[/list]
