[h1]Chaos Faster Turn — Speed Up AI Turns[/h1]

Tired of sitting through slow-motion enemy movement and animations while the AI takes its turn? This mod lets you speed up enemy (AI) turn playback on the fly — movement, shooting, idle animations, all of it — with simple hotkeys. It also lets you speed up your [b]own[/b] unit's movement while it's walking on your own turn, with the same hotkeys.

[h2]What It Does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Speed up the [b]AI's turn[/b] — up to x20 by default (configurable) — with the same hotkeys always available at normal speed the rest of your own turn
[*]Speed up your [b]own unit's movement[/b] on your turn too — hold Space (or tap +/-) while a unit is walking to zip it to its destination faster; every move starts back at normal speed
[*]Steps go x1 → x1.5 → x2 → x3 → x4 → … up to the configured max
[*]Two on-screen indicators show the current speed whenever it isn't x1.
[*]Automatically drops back to normal speed whenever the enemy attacks one of your units, and restores your chosen speed right after — you never miss the moment an enemy actually hurts your squad
[/list]

[h2]Controls (during the enemy's turn or during player unit movement)[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]=/+[/b] (Equals/Plus) — increase enemy turn speed by one step
[*][b]-[/b] (Minus) — decrease enemy turn speed by one step (never goes below normal)
[*][b]Backspace[/b] — reset straight back to normal speed (x1)
[*][b]Space[/b] — toggle: instantly drop to x1, press again to jump right back to whatever speed you had before
[/list]

[h2]Controls (during your own unit's movement)[/h2]
The exact same four keys, but only while one of your own units is actively moving — they do nothing at any other point on your turn, and every new move always starts at normal speed:
[list]
[*][b]Space[/b] — jump straight to x2 movement speed (or back to whatever speed you'd set on this move, if you press it again)
[*][b]=/+[/b] (Equals/Plus) — increase this move's speed by one step
[*][b]-[/b] (Minus) — decrease this move's speed by one step (never goes below normal)
[*][b]Backspace[/b] — reset this move straight back to normal speed (x1)
[/list]

[h2]Configuration[/h2]
Available in the mod settings (main menu → Mods → Chaos Faster Turn):
[list]
[*][b]Max Speed Multiplier[/b] — upper bound the speed can be increased to, for both the enemy turn and your own movement speed-up (default: 20)
[*][b]Reset Speed On Player Turn[/b] — when enabled, speed automatically drops back to x1 once your turn starts, so every AI turn begins at normal speed again; when disabled, whatever speed you last set carries over turn after turn. Either way, [b]Space[/b] can always instantly recall the last speed you used, even after it's been auto-reset (default: on)
[*][b]Reset Speed On Enemy Attack[/b] — when enabled, speed automatically drops to x1 for any enemy ability used against one of your units (ranged, melee, grenades, mind control, anything) and restores automatically right after; as a backstop, it also drops to x1 briefly any time one of your units actually takes damage during the enemy's turn, even from something indirect like an explosion or a status effect. Either way you never miss seeing an enemy hurt your squad at high speed. When disabled, enemy attacks on you play at whatever speed is currently selected (default: on)
[/list]
